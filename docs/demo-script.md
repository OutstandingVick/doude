# Feasibility3D — 3-minute demo

> Narrated walkthrough. Times are cumulative from the start; the click target is
> noted for each beat, followed by the one sentence to say.

**0:00 — The problem.** *(Dashboard `/`, point at the header.)*
"Operators self-report spill volumes in the Niger Delta, and the disputed number
is what compensation is argued over — regulators have no independent check."

**0:25 — The data.** *(Scroll to "Sensor readings", point at flow and pressure.*)
"We never touch the operator's SCADA — we start from two pressure sensors and
two flow meters, one at each end of the pipeline, read every minute."

**0:45 — Run analysis.** *(Click "Run analysis" in the sidebar.)*
"The engine reconstitutes everything from those four signals — and it just
detected the leak five minutes after it opened."

**1:10 — The map.** *(Look at the KPI cards, then the "Spill map".)*
"It located the breach at kilometre 13.39, within 325 metres, and already drew
the surface slick six hours out."

**1:35 — Quantification.** *(Open the spill, click the "Quantification" tab.)*
"Two independent methods agree within 0.2%: 28,880 barrels released, mass balance
cross-checked against a steady-state estimate."

**2:00 — The sealed report.** *(Click "Evidence & hashes", then "Verify now".)*
"Every result is sealed as a SHA-256 report hash — re-run it on the same file and
the hash is reproduced exactly, so nothing can be quietly edited."

**2:20 — Data management.** *(Click "Advance to JIV", then the "Evidence" and
"Audit log" tabs.)*
"The regulator advances the case JIV, drops a field photo into evidence, and the
audit log shows the whole chain is intact — every action tamper-evident."

**2:40 — Form A.** *(Click "Regulatory forms", then "Generate draft".)*
"The operator gets a draft Form A notification with the 24-hour deadline already
stamped, ready to check against the official template."

**2:55 — Close.**
"Independent, repeatable, and tamper-evident — that's Feasibility3D."
